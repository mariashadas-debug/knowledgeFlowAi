# ParcelFlow FAQ

## Can I change my address?

Yes, before dispatch. Contact support with the order number and corrected address. Address changes
after dispatch depend on the carrier and are not guaranteed.

## When will a refund arrive?

An approved refund normally reaches the original payment method within five business days.

## How long is standard delivery?

Standard domestic delivery normally takes three to five business days after dispatch.

## Does ParcelFlow use OCR?

No. KnowledgeFlow can extract text-based PDFs, TXT, and Markdown files. Image-only PDFs require a
future OCR integration.
