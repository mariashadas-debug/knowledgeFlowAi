# ParcelFlow Delivery Policy

Standard domestic delivery normally takes three to five business days after dispatch. Express
delivery normally takes one to two business days. Delivery estimates exclude public holidays and
weather-related carrier disruptions.

Customers receive a tracking link by email when the parcel leaves the warehouse. If tracking has
not changed for 72 hours before the estimated delivery date, customers should wait; carrier scans
may be delayed. After the estimated date, support can open a carrier trace.

ParcelFlow permits one delivery-address correction before dispatch. Once a parcel is in transit,
the carrier's redirect rules apply and a redirect cannot be guaranteed.
