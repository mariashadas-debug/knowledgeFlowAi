# ParcelFlow Refund Policy

## Damaged parcels

Customers should report visible parcel damage within 48 hours of delivery and include clear photos
of the packaging and item. After photo verification, ParcelFlow approves eligible damaged-parcel
refunds. Approved refunds reach the original payment method within five business days.

## Lost parcels

A parcel is considered lost when tracking has not updated for seven calendar days after the
estimated delivery date. Support may then issue a replacement or refund at the customer's choice.

## Exclusions

Shipping fees are refundable only when ParcelFlow caused the delivery failure. Personalized items
are not refundable unless they arrived damaged or materially different from the order.
